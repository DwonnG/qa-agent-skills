# Epic Analysis

Analyze the epic for comprehensive planning:

## Scope and Objectives
- Business goals and success criteria
- User value proposition and outcomes
- Market and competitive considerations

## Story Breakdown Strategy
- Recommended story decomposition
- Delivery sequencing and prioritization
- Dependencies and integration points

## Release Planning
- MVP definition and phased delivery
- Risk mitigation and validation strategies
- Stakeholder communication and alignment

## Success Metrics
- Key performance indicators
- User adoption and feedback mechanisms
- Business impact measurement

Format as a comprehensive epic analysis with strategic recommendations.
